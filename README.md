## About Me

<table>
<tr>
<td width="55%" valign="top">

Hi, I’m Kyann. I learn best by making things work.

I build stuff. Useful stuff, mostly.

I am a 21-year-old Computer Science student at Adamson University, based in Manila and Cavite, and I enjoy the moment when a messy idea finally starts making sense.

If I don’t understand something, I keep pulling at the thread until I do. I read, ask questions, break things, and rebuild them. That curiosity keeps taking me toward full-stack development, systems architecture, data, and interfaces that don’t make people work harder than they need to.

Code isn’t the whole story, though. Through student leadership, I’ve led programs for 100+ students and managed organization budgets where every peso had to be explained. Those experiences taught me to listen first, communicate clearly, and stay useful when the plan changes—skills that are just as important to building good software as the code itself.
